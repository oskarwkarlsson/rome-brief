Tiramisu & family trattorias

You asked for mom-and-pop rooms and serious tiramisù. These are verified family trattorias, spread around the city, plus four tiramisù stops that are useful on this trip. Book the trattorie — the good small rooms fill up. Hours are given only where a venue or official page states them; call if anything looks tight.

Family-run trattorias

Near the hotel · Esquilino

Trattoria da Danilo

Via Petrarca 13 · a short walk from Hotel Madison · +39 06 77200111 · trattoriadadanilo.com

Danilo Valente opened this small room with his mother, Lina, in the kitchen — the definition of a family trattoria a few streets from Piazza Vittorio. The carbonara is the dish people talk about; the rest of the Roman list (cacio e pepe, trippa, seasonal coratella) is the real reason to go.

This weekend: closed Sunday, and closed Monday lunch, per their site. When they are open, recent listings give lunch 13:00–15:00 and dinner 20:00–23:00 — confirm those times when you book. A strong Friday or Saturday dinner if you want to stay close to the hotel.

Centro · Pantheon

Armando al Pantheon

Salita de’ Crescenzi 31 · armandoalpantheon.it

The Gargioli family has cooked Roman food here since 1961, in a small room a few metres from the Pantheon. This is the centro lunch to book if a table exists: traditional, unshowy, and still a family house.

Order: the Roman primi and whatever is in season (carciofi, coda, a proper pasta).

This weekend: lunch 12:30–15:00 (including Saturday). Dinner Monday–Friday 18:00–23:00. Closed Saturday evening and all day Sunday. Book only on their site — they do not take email reservations, and they are often full 30 days ahead. Worth trying for Friday dinner or Saturday lunch; have a backup.

Trastevere

Checco Er Carettiere

Via Benedetta 10, just behind Piazza Trilussa · +39 06 581 7018 · checcoercarettiere.it

Open since 1935 and still run by Checco’s granddaughters — three generations of Roman cooking, homemade pasta and a quiet internal courtyard off the noisiest streets of Trastevere.

Order: a classic Roman pasta, then a braise or seasonal vegetables. There are more than 300 wines if you want to linger.

This weekend: daily 12:00–15:00 and 19:00–24:00, so it is the reliable Sunday dinner in Trastevere. Book.

Testaccio

Felice a Testaccio

Via Mastro Giorgio 29 · +39 06 574 6800 · feliceatestaccio.com

The Trivelloni family has been here since 1936. The original Testaccio room is still the one to book — they have later opened in Milan, Turin and a small “On The Go” in Via delle Carrozze, but this is the house that made their name. The cacio e pepe is finished at the table, in the traditional mantecatura.

Order: cacio e pepe, then abbacchio or polpette if you still have room.

This weekend: daily, lunch 12:30–15:30, dinner 19:00–23:30. Ideal Sunday lunch after the Ethica Christmas Village around the corner. Book ahead — it is popular.

Prati

Da Dante

Via Monte Santo 36 · +39 06 372 0852 · ristorantedadante.it

A loved Prati trattoria since 1952, still in the family (the site introduces Armando, Micaela, Martina, Francesco and Marco). White tablecloths, generous portions, recipes passed down the generations. It sits in residential Prati, a walk or short bus from the Vatican rather than on the tourist doorstep.

Order: a Roman classic or the house ragù, and do not rush.

This weekend: Monday–Saturday 12:45–15:00 and 19:45–02:00; Sunday lunch only, 12:45–15:00. A good Friday dinner in the same neighbourhood as Alexanderplatz, if you eat before the jazz.

Trastevere · closed tonight

Da Gildo

Via della Scala 31/A · +39 06 580 0733 · dagildoatrastevere.com

Three generations in the same Trastevere room since 1956, when Egisto “Gildo” opened it and his sons carried it on. A straightforward family trattoria, not a scene.

Order: carciofi alla romana or alla giudia in season, and cacio e pepe.

This weekend: 12:00–15:30 and 19:00–23:30, closed Thursday — so not tonight, but Friday to Sunday are open. Book by phone.

Near-hotel backups already on this page, and worth a call tonight: Trattoria Cecio and Trattoria Vecchia Roma. They are neighbourhood rooms; confirm the family character and a table when you telephone.

Four tiramisù stops

Spagna · centro

Pompi · Via della Croce

Via della Croce 88 · near the Spanish Steps · +39 06 2430 4431 · barpompi.it

“Il regno del tiramisù” since 1960, and still the Roman reference for a classic takeaway cup, with pistachio and strawberry alongside the traditional. It is a small, well-known group, not a hidden trattoria, but the Croce shop is the convenient stop on a Saturday walk through the centro.

Order: the classic, then a second flavour if you have the appetite.

This weekend: Sunday–Thursday 10:00–22:00; Friday and Saturday 10:00–22:30.

Prati · after the Vatican

Pompi · Piazza Risorgimento

Piazza Risorgimento 43 · a few minutes from the Vatican walls · +39 06 3973 7088 · barpompi.it

The same house, in the most useful place on Friday: sit down for a tiramisù after the Museums and Pizzarium, before you head back to the hotel or on to jazz. There is also a newer Pompi at Via di Tor Millina 33, by Piazza Navona, Sunday–Thursday 10:00–23:00 and Friday–Saturday 10:00–23:30. Navona shop.

This weekend at Risorgimento: Thursday 08:30–23:00; Friday and Saturday 08:00–00:00; Sunday 09:30–22:30.

Piazza Navona

Two Sizes

Via del Governo Vecchio 88 · +39 06 6476 1191 · twosizestiramisu.com

Paolo and Francesco, two friends from Calabria, do one thing: tiramisù to go, in a small or a large cup, made without alcohol. Official prices are €3 (90 g) and €4 (130 g). Flavours on their site: classico, pistachio, caramel, peanut butter and strawberry. Eat it walking toward Piazza Navona.

Order: the classico first; pistachio is the usual second.

This weekend: their own site does not list hours. Recent listings, including Tourism Roma (checked 9 October 2026), give Tuesday–Sunday 11:00–22:00, closed Monday — so Thursday to Sunday should be fine, but confirm at the door. Tourism Roma note.

Trastevere

Merisù

Via di San Francesco a Ripa 29 · +39 06 6941 5704 · roma-trastevere.merisutiramisu.com

Tiramisù made in front of you, layer by layer, with savoiardi and coffee from a Neapolitan moka. The house “Merisù” flavour uses zabaione; they also do pistachio, a gluten-free classic, and a lactose-free / vegan version. A specialist shop rather than a trattoria — useful after a Trastevere dinner, and open late.

Order: Tradizionale or the house Merisù, prepared on the spot.

This weekend: Thursday and Sunday 10:30–24:00; Friday and Saturday 10:30–00:30.

Friday 27 · Vatican morning

This is the day to do the Museums. They open at 08:00; the New Wing closes from 13:00 to 17:30 today, so an early slot really matters. Showers are likely — the Vatican is mostly indoors.

  1. 08:00 · Vatican Museums

    Book a skip-the-line ticket on the official site for 08:00: tickets.museivaticani.va. Open 08:00–20:00, last entry 18:00. Full official ticket is €20, plus €5 to book skip-the-line online. Metro A from Termini to Ottaviano or Cipro; allow plenty of time. Hours and the New Wing closure.

  2. Late morning · St Peter’s Basilica

    After the Museums, walk over to the Basilica. In this season it is open 07:00–19:00. The dome is on winter hours, 07:30–17:00. Cover shoulders and knees. On Monday 30 Nov the Basilica has a temporary morning closure — you will already have left, but don’t plan a leftover visit then. Official hours.

  3. Lunch in Prati

    Pizzarium Bonci, Via della Meloria 43, is the classic Vatican-neighbourhood pizza al taglio. Official hours Friday: 11:00–22:00. Go early or expect a queue. Then walk to Pompi on Piazza Risorgimento for tiramisù (open Friday 08:00–00:00) — it is a few minutes from the Vatican walls. There is also a Bonci counter on the ground floor of Termini, 07:00–21:30 every day, if you want a slice later by the hotel.

  4. Afternoon · menswear in Prati

    Shopping is a good Friday plan even if it rains — the shops are indoors. After Pizzarium and a tiramisù at Pompi on Piazza Risorgimento, start next door at Cerruti Roma dal 1948, Via Cola di Rienzo 46 (Monday–Saturday 10:30–19:30, closed Sunday). Then walk the length of the street toward the river for more ready-to-wear, casual and formal. Full walking order, hours and a note on alterations are in the Menswear shopping section. Leave enough time to be at dinner or jazz in the evening; the street itself is an easy walk from Da Dante and Alexanderplatz.

  5. Evening

    If you want a family trattoria in the same neighbourhood, book Da Dante in Via Monte Santo (dinner from 19:45, open late). Eat first, then walk or taxi to jazz. Best music fit after a Vatican day: Joe Locke again at Alexanderplatz, Via Ostia 9, 20:00–00:00 — it is already in Prati. Reserve. If you would rather stay near the hotel, book Da Danilo instead (open Friday; closed Sunday).

    Or a concert: Daniel Harding conducts Santa Cecilia in Strauss’s Don Juan and Four Last Songs (soprano Rachel Willis-Sørensen) and Brahms 4, Friday 20:00 at Auditorium Parco della Musica. Tickets €23–€54. Official page. Casa del Jazz also has the CDJO / Mario Corvini “Bob Brookmeyer” programme at 19:30, €15, Viale di Porta Ardeatina 55. Casa del Jazz.

Saturday 28 · the clear, cold day

The best weather of the stay: almost dry, but cooler, with a wind warning. Use it for walking the centro or the ancient city, and dip into the new Christmas village at Villa Borghese if you want festive atmosphere.

  1. Morning · Tridente menswear, or the Colosseum

    If shopping is the plan, this is the day for the Tridente. You can start near the hotel at Socrate, Via Nazionale 89 (Saturday 09:30–13:00, then 15:30–19:30), and walk on to Piazza del Popolo for a downhill loop of Via del Babuino and Via dei Condotti — Battistoni, Brioni, Boggi, Gente and Eddy Monetti. Details and a walking order are in Menswear shopping. The other shape for the day is still a slow centro loop or official tickets for the Colosseum / Forum (Metro B from Termini to Colosseo). You probably cannot do a full shop and a full ancient-city visit in one morning; pick one, and keep Christmas World for later if you have the legs.

  2. Lunch

    If you are already on Via dei Condotti, pause mid-loop at Pompi on Via della Croce (Saturday 10:00–22:30) for coffee and tiramisù — it sits one street north of Condotti. If you can get a table, Armando al Pantheon is the family-run Saturday lunch in the centro (12:30–15:00; they close Saturday evening and Sunday, and book only online — often full). Otherwise Monti is the easiest neighbourhood from the hotel, or eat at Da Danilo near the hotel (open Saturday, closed Sunday).

  3. Afternoon · Christmas World, Villa Borghese

    The city’s big Christmas park opens today, 10:00–20:00, at Viale del Galoppatoio, Villa Borghese, through 10 January 2027. Off-peak adult tickets start at €12.50; rides and food are extra. Plan your visit · tickets via the official Christmas World site. A calmer alternative in Testaccio: Ethica Christmas Village at Città dell’Altra Economia, Largo Dino Frisullo, Saturday 10:00–20:00. ethica-village.it.

  4. Evening

    Harding’s Santa Cecilia programme has a slightly earlier Saturday start, 18:00, which leaves room for a late dinner. Tickets.

    Other official listings: Francesca Michielin at Auditorium Conciliazione, Via della Conciliazione 4 — handy if you are already near the Vatican end of town. Confirm the start time on the venue page or TicketOne. Last night of Joe Locke at Alexanderplatz, 20:00–00:00. Shiva is also at Palazzo dello Sport tonight (and Friday and Sunday). TicketOne.

Menswear shopping

Friday afternoon after the Vatican, or Saturday, or both. These streets are made for ready-to-wear, casual and formal. A full bespoke commission at a house like Battistoni or Brioni needs several fittings over weeks, and you leave on Sunday — so buy off the rack and ask about alterations (sleeves, trousers, a jacket waist). Socrate on Via Nazionale has an in-house workshop for exactly that. Hours below are from official shop pages where we have them; where a brand does not publish them, we say so.

Two updates before you set off: Wools is no longer on Via del Governo Vecchio — the boutique moved to Geneva. And the official George’s site now lists a women’s boutique at Via Della Rotonda 5, not a men’s shop at Via del Pantheon 58, so skip George’s for menswear on this trip.

Friday afternoon · Prati, Via Cola di Rienzo

The street starts at Piazza Risorgimento, next to the Vatican walls, and runs toward the river. You will already be in the neighbourhood after the Museums, Pizzarium and Pompi. Shops below are open Friday afternoon; Cerruti closes at 19:30, so start soon after lunch.

  1. Lunch and a first stop

    Pizza at Pizzarium, Via della Meloria 43 (Friday 11:00–22:00), then coffee and tiramisù at Pompi, Piazza Risorgimento 43 (Friday 08:00–00:00). Cerruti is essentially next door.

  2. 1. Cerruti Roma dal 1948 · Via Cola di Rienzo 46

    A classic Prati men’s boutique since 1948 — the place to start for formal and tailored ready-to-wear. Official hours: Monday–Saturday 10:30–19:30 continuous; closed Sunday. +39 06 321 6793 · WhatsApp +39 345 022 6845 · cerrutiviacoladirienzo.it

  3. 2. Walk east along Via Cola di Rienzo

    This is Prati’s main shopping street, a mix of Italian casual and more formal rooms. Browse as you go; you do not need a list for every doorway.

  4. 3. Optional shirts · Il Camiciaio, Via Fabio Massimo 43

    A short detour off the mid-stretch of Cola di Rienzo. They have made shirts, ties, knitwear and trousers in-house since 1963. Official hours: Monday–Saturday 10:00–19:30. ilcamiciaio.it

  5. 4. Fellini Boutique Uomo · Via Cola di Rienzo 281

    Toward the river end of the street, a local men’s boutique for a more contemporary look. Official hours: Monday–Saturday 10:00–20:00 (Sunday is split hours, which does not matter this afternoon). +39 06 321 0629 · felliniroma.it

  6. 5. Dan John · Via Cola di Rienzo 288

    Casual and smart-casual Italian menswear, a few doors on. Official store hours: Monday–Saturday 10:00–20:00. +39 06 687 2982 · Dan John store page

From here you are still in Prati for Friday evening: dinner at Da Dante, or jazz at Alexanderplatz. Metro A from Ottaviano or Lepanto takes you back to Termini.

Saturday · the Tridente

Piazza del Popolo, Piazza di Spagna and the streets between them — Via del Babuino, Via dei Condotti, Via Borgognona. A compact, downhill loop. Battistoni’s official close is 19:00, so do not start too late if that is the main call.

  1. Optional first, near the hotel · Socrate, Via Nazionale 89

    A short walk down Via Nazionale from Termini, also with an entrance on Via dei Serpenti 70. Classic and casual menswear since 1956, plus an in-house workshop that will alter what you buy — the most practical place on this trip if a jacket or pair of trousers needs a tweak before Sunday. Official hours on socrate.co.it: Monday 15:30–19:30; Tuesday–Saturday 09:30–13:00 and 15:30–19:30. On Saturday they close for lunch, so go at opening or after 15:30 on the way home. +39 06 484530.

  2. Then start at Piazza del Popolo and walk down Via del Babuino

    Metro A to Flaminio, or a walk from Socrate toward Spagna and turn up to Popolo so you shop downhill.

    Gente Roma, Via del Babuino 185, near the Popolo end — a long-standing multi-brand room for a more fashion-forward look. Their site genteroma.com does not publish boutique hours; write to support@genteroma.it or confirm at the door. Recent directories list Tuesday–Saturday 10:00–19:30, but treat that as unverified until you see the sign.

    Boggi Milano, Via del Babuino 38/40, near Piazza di Spagna — approachable Italian ready-to-wear, useful for a jacket, shirt or knit you can wear this weekend. Official address and booking: boggi.com, +39 06 3265 1256, rm.babuino@boggi.it. The brand does not print hours clearly on that page; the shop is widely listed as daily 10:30–20:00 — confirm when you book or at the door.

  3. Coffee and tiramisù · Pompi, Via della Croce 88

    One street north of Condotti, a natural mid-loop pause. Official Saturday hours 10:00–22:30. barpompi.it. If you would rather a sit-down lunch, Armando al Pantheon is a 10–15 minute walk south — only if you already have a table.

  4. Via dei Condotti, starting at the Spanish Steps

    Battistoni, Via dei Condotti 61/A — a Roman house, 100% made in Italy, and the most “gentleman” stop on the street. Ready-to-wear in the shop; skip a new bespoke order. Official hours: Monday–Saturday 10:00–19:00, closed Sunday. +39 06 697 6111 · battistoni.com

    Brioni, Via dei Condotti 21/A, further down toward Via del Corso — formal tailoring at a high level, again ready-to-wear only on this timetable. Official store page: brioni.com. The site did not display opening hours when checked; call the boutique or confirm on the door. +39 06 678 3428 is the number widely published for this shop.

  5. One street south · Eddy Monetti, Via Borgognona 36

    Neapolitan menswear in a quieter street running parallel to Condotti. Official boutique listing: eddymonetti.com, +39 06 679 4117, roma@eddymonetti.com. The official page gives the address but not the hours — call or confirm at the door before you detour.

If you still have energy, Christmas World at Villa Borghese is a 15–20 minute walk up from Piazza del Popolo and stays open until 20:00. Two Sizes, on Via del Governo Vecchio 88, remains a good tiramisù stop if you drift toward Piazza Navona — that is the street Wools used to occupy.

Practical tips

Getting around from Termini

The hotel sits on Via Marsala, on the north side of Roma Termini, so Metro A and B are underneath you. Metro A west to Ottaviano or Cipro for the Vatican; Metro B south to Colosseo. Monti is a short walk; the centro is walkable if you want the long way, or a couple of metro stops.

Official ATAC fares: BIT €1.50, valid 100 minutes (unlimited bus/tram, one metro ride); Roma 24H €8.50; 48H €15; 72H €22, each from first validation. Buy in the ATAC app, at machines, or tap a contactless bank card (tap & go) for BIT fares. Validate, and keep the ticket. These tickets are not valid on the non-stop Trenitalia or Cotral airport trains to Fiumicino. BIT · 72H.
