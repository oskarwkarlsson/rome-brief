Tonight · Thursday 26

You land around 19:00. Keep dinner close, classic and booked if you can. After a travel day, a 5–10 minute walk beats a long taxi in the rain.

Top pick · 5–10 min walk

Trattoria Cecio

Via Principe Amedeo 104 · trattoriacecio.it · +39 06 446 4991

Proper Roman cooking a short walk from the hotel. The trattoria says it is open every day for lunch and dinner; it is small, and they recommend booking. Order carbonara or cacio e pepe. Dinner has been listed until 22:45 — confirm when you call.

If you want a slightly bigger night

Trattoria Vecchia Roma

Via Ferruccio 12/b/c, Esquilino · about 15 minutes on foot · trattoriavecchiaroma.it · +39 06 446 7143

Open Monday–Saturday, dinner 19:00–23:00; closed Sunday. Aim for a table around 20:30. A busy, much-loved neighbourhood place.

Backup

Taverna dei Migliori · Viale Manzoni 107

A little further towards Manzoni, useful if the first two are full. Call to confirm tonight’s hours before you walk over.

If you still have energy after dinner: vibraphonist Joe Locke’s quintet with Rosario Giuliani plays at Alexanderplatz Jazz Club, Via Ostia 9 (Prati), 20:00–00:00 tonight, tomorrow and Saturday. It is a metro or taxi ride from Termini, so only go if you are not wiped out. Reserve.

Friday 27 · Vatican morning

This is the day to do the Museums. They open at 08:00; the New Wing closes from 13:00 to 17:30 today, so an early slot really matters. Showers are likely — the Vatican is mostly indoors.

  1. 08:00 · Vatican Museums

    Book a skip-the-line ticket on the official site for 08:00: tickets.museivaticani.va. Open 08:00–20:00, last entry 18:00. Full official ticket is €20, plus €5 to book skip-the-line online. Metro A from Termini to Ottaviano or Cipro; allow plenty of time. Hours and the New Wing closure.

  2. Late morning · St Peter’s Basilica

    After the Museums, walk over to the Basilica. In this season it is open 07:00–19:00. The dome is on winter hours, 07:30–17:00. Cover shoulders and knees. On Monday 30 Nov the Basilica has a temporary morning closure — you will already have left, but don’t plan a leftover visit then. Official hours.

  3. Lunch in Prati

    Pizzarium Bonci, Via della Meloria 43, is the classic Vatican-neighbourhood pizza al taglio. Official hours Friday: 11:00–22:00. Go early or expect a queue. There is also a Bonci counter on the ground floor of Termini, 07:00–21:30 every day, if you want a slice later by the hotel.

  4. Afternoon

    If the rain persists, go back to the hotel and rest, or browse Monti (a short walk from Termini) under cover of porticoes and cafés. If it clears, a slow walk through Prati and back is enough — save the big outdoor sights for Saturday.

  5. Evening

    Best fit after a Vatican day: Joe Locke again at Alexanderplatz, Via Ostia 9, 20:00–00:00 — it is already in Prati. Reserve.

    Or a concert: Daniel Harding conducts Santa Cecilia in Strauss’s Don Juan and Four Last Songs (soprano Rachel Willis-Sørensen) and Brahms 4, Friday 20:00 at Auditorium Parco della Musica. Tickets €23–€54. Official page. Casa del Jazz also has the CDJO / Mario Corvini “Bob Brookmeyer” programme at 19:30, €15, Viale di Porta Ardeatina 55. Casa del Jazz.

Saturday 28 · the clear, cold day

The best weather of the stay: almost dry, but cooler, with a wind warning. Use it for walking the centro or the ancient city, and dip into the new Christmas village at Villa Borghese if you want festive atmosphere.

  1. Morning · centro or the Colosseum

    Two good shapes for the day. Either a slow loop of Trevi, the Pantheon and Piazza Navona, with coffee at Sant’Eustachio or Tazza d’Oro, or book official tickets for the Colosseum / Forum if you want the ancient city (Metro B from Termini to Colosseo). Check times and book only on the official ticketing site before you go.

  2. Lunch

    Monti is the easiest neighbourhood from the hotel — pizza at Alle Carrette, or a trattoria if you book. If you are already in the centro, Roscioli or a nearby tavola calda beats a long detour.

  3. Afternoon · Christmas World, Villa Borghese

    The city’s big Christmas park opens today, 10:00–20:00, at Viale del Galoppatoio, Villa Borghese, through 10 January 2027. Off-peak adult tickets start at €12.50; rides and food are extra. Plan your visit · tickets via the official Christmas World site. A calmer alternative in Testaccio: Ethica Christmas Village at Città dell’Altra Economia, Largo Dino Frisullo, Saturday 10:00–20:00. ethica-village.it.

  4. Evening

    Harding’s Santa Cecilia programme has a slightly earlier Saturday start, 18:00, which leaves room for a late dinner. Tickets.

    Other official listings: Francesca Michielin at Auditorium Conciliazione, Via della Conciliazione 4 — handy if you are already near the Vatican end of town. Confirm the start time on the venue page or TicketOne. Last night of Joe Locke at Alexanderplatz, 20:00–00:00. Shiva is also at Palazzo dello Sport tonight (and Friday and Sunday). TicketOne.

Sunday 29 · markets, music, an easy close

A clear, cold day. Skip the free Vatican Museums morning unless you love a very long queue: they are free 09:00–14:00, last entry 12:30, and you cannot book ahead. St Peter’s is still open 07:00–19:00 if you missed it on Friday.

  1. Late morning · Testaccio or Trastevere

    Ethica Christmas Village is open 10:00–20:00. Combine it with a wander through Testaccio and a proper lunch, or spend the day in Trastevere: slower streets, pizza, and gelato at Fior di Luna if it is open. The EUR Christmas village by the lake (Viale Oceania / Viale America) also opened on Friday and runs to 10 January — confirm hours on @natalealleur or Turismo Roma before crossing the city for it.

  2. A free cultural afternoon

    arciMACROsuperQUEER at MACRO, Via Nizza 138, is a free festival today and yesterday: exhibitions, talks, performances and concerts. Some talks need a free Eventbrite booking; the exhibitions do not.

  3. If you want one more concert

    ANOHNI — “Wilderness”, 20:00, Sala Santa Cecilia at Auditorium Parco della Musica, from €50. Her only Italian date, with piano, percussion and guitar. Auditorium page. Quieter options at Casa del Jazz: Lucia Bova (harp) at 12:00, Beatrice Gatto at 18:00, both €15. At Alexanderplatz: Demetra Bellina 11:30–14:30, then Alberto Laurenti “Napoli prima e dopo”, 20:00–00:00.

Monti

The closest “proper” neighbourhood: 10–15 minutes on foot from the hotel, downhill towards the Forum. Good for Saturday lunch or a Friday afternoon if the Vatican wore you out.

  • Alle Carrette — Via della Madonna dei Monti 95. A long-standing neighbourhood pizzeria. Check hours and expect a wait at peak times.
  • Monti is also full of wine bars and small trattorie around Via Panisperna and Via dei Serpenti. Book, and be wary of menus printed only in English with photos.

Centro

  • Sant’Eustachio Il Caffè — Piazza di Sant’Eustachio 82. The classic centro espresso, a short walk from the Pantheon. caffesanteustachio.com.
  • Tazza d’Oro — Via degli Orfani 84, also by the Pantheon. Another historic roastery; granita in warmer months, espresso always.
  • For a sit-down lunch in the centro, book well ahead (Armando al Pantheon and Roscioli are the usual names, and they fill). Otherwise grab pizza bianca and keep walking.

Near the Vatican · Prati

  • Pizzarium Bonci — Via della Meloria 43. Mon–Sat 11:00–22:00; Sunday 11:00–15:00 and 17:00–22:00. Official hours.
  • Alexanderplatz is also here if you stay in the area for Friday night jazz.

Trastevere & Testaccio

Save these for Sunday, or a Saturday evening if you are not at a concert. Trastevere is busy and touristy at night; walk a street off the main squares. Fior di Luna (Via della Lungaretta) is a well-known gelato stop — check it is open before you detour. Testaccio pairs naturally with Ethica Christmas Village and has a more local lunch scene; book a trattoria rather than arriving hungry with no plan.

Practical tips

Getting around from Termini

The hotel sits on Via Marsala, on the north side of Roma Termini, so Metro A and B are underneath you. Metro A west to Ottaviano or Cipro for the Vatican; Metro B south to Colosseo. Monti is a short walk; the centro is walkable if you want the long way, or a couple of metro stops.

Official ATAC fares: BIT €1.50, valid 100 minutes (unlimited bus/tram, one metro ride); Roma 24H €8.50; 48H €15; 72H €22, each from first validation. Buy in the ATAC app, at machines, or tap a contactless bank card (tap & go) for BIT fares. Validate, and keep the ticket. These tickets are not valid on the non-stop Trenitalia or Cotral airport trains to Fiumicino. BIT · 72H.
